MASS ESTIMATE REVISION — supplied parts guides incorporated
=========================================================

1. Evidence hierarchy
----------------------
The supplied PCB guide (Rev D, 2026-11-01) gives the parts list, four XC330
locations, battery options and four-group motor-power concept. The supplied
upper-body connection/mounting guide (2026-10-29) gives PCB sizes and proposed
mounting regions. Their schematic illustrations are not scale mounting CAD.
Catalog weights are used for identified hardware; everything else has explicit
nominal/low/high estimates in CSV. No physical parts were weighed.

2. Existing printed geometry
-----------------------------
All existing printed parts now have explicit net CAD volume, material density
and effective material fraction. Source mass values were approximately volume
in m3 at 1 kg/m3; these net volumes matched independent mesh-volume checks in
the initial review. Hollow head volume is already hollow: no second hollowing
factor is applied to the outer bounding sphere.
- PETG Basic assumption:1250 kg/m3 for structural components and feet.
- PLA assumption:1240 kg/m3 for head shell/ears/nose.
- Thin brackets/links and head walls:nominal effective fraction0.95, range0.85-1.
- Bulk torso rails/plates:nominal0.85, range0.65-1.
- Feet:nominal0.65, range0.40-1.
Effective fraction is the material amount relative to the CAD net solid volume,
INCLUDING perimeters, top/bottom layers and infill. It is NOT a slicer infill
setting. These fractions are planning assumptions, not a print-process model.
Uniformly scaling CAD inertia approximates the nonuniform effect of infill.
Use slicer grams or measured finished-part mass when available.

The two 23x40x3mm parts named bearing_roll are mounting plates with large holes,
not whole bearings. Actual mesh inspection corrected their previous steel
classification to printed PETG. Two separate roll-bearing reserves are included
at3g each, plus two explicit16x22x4mm yaw bearings at4g each. Exact bearing models
are not confirmed. Total14g; planning range9-22g. Remove an extra reserve if its
bearing is already represented by a purchased assembly.

3. Motor masses and the missing 2-4 joints
-----------------------------------------
PCB guide p1 assigns XC330-M288-T to left/right hip pitch and knee pitch. Each is
23g; remaining modeled motors are XL330-M288-T at18g. Thus12 modeled motors:
4 XC + 8 XL =236g. The earlier generic20g XC upgrade allowance was REMOVED;
there is no double counting. Detailed neck motor parts share a total18g EACH.
Motor CoM/inertia remains based on source geometry with uniformly scaled mass;
this is not a manufacturer's complete motor inertia tensor. Mesh appearance
has not been replaced with exact XC330 CAD.
The guide plans14-16 motors. Extra2-4 XL motor masses add36-72g but their brackets,
links and actual moving attachment locations are unspecified. Disabled rows in
additional_masses.csv expose this reserve without creating fictitious joints.

6. Motor-power electronics: current design remains open
-------------------------------------------------------
Guide pp11-16 discusses four independent motor-power groups and a custom
16-port distribution PCB. To avoid pretending that a single10g converter is the
whole supply, budget:
- Four converter mass reserves15g each,60g total, based on D42V110F5 size/mass.
  This is a MASS reference only. The guide leaves final models/count unresolved.
- Logic converter candidate D24V50F5:3g, without optional headers.
- Custom distribution PCB with board-mounted connectors/fuseholders:25g.
- Additional off-board protection/switch/cutoff hardware:15g.
Total power subsystem103g; range66-181g. Wiring counted elsewhere.
This mass update does not approve the reference converter for the intended
voltage/current/thermal conditions. Power topology and packaging need the
team's separate electrical design and mounting checks.

7. Position and uncertainty
----------------------------
Masses already in CAD retain their source CoM. Added parts use proposed mounting
regions:camera follows head, battery/converters/boards follow torso, paw shells
follow feet, leg harness/fasteners follow corresponding links. Box inertias are
combined through the parallel-axis theorem. Extra rows have no collision or
visual geometry, so this does not establish fit or cable routing.
CSV low/high columns give alternative planning bounds. Summing them gives a
budget envelope, not a statistical confidence interval. Nominal URDF contains
one chosen estimate, not all scenarios at once.
